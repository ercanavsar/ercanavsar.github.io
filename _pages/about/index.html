I am a researcher at DTU Aqua, the National Institute of Aquatic Resources at the Technical University of Denmark, where I develop computer vision methods for fisheries and marine applications.

My work focuses on turning underwater and on-board imagery into usable data: detecting, counting and measuring fish, and making these models reliable enough for the messy conditions of real surveys and commercial vessels.

Before joining DTU, I worked at Dokuz Eylul University and Cukurova University in Turkey.

Collaboration: I am always glad to hear from people interested in working together. If you are a student looking for a thesis or internship project, or a researcher or practitioner with a problem where computer vision might help, feel free to email me.
